Bathroom Music Knob

Simulator: the knob's own screen code, running in the browser

Voice search

Open Search on the knob. While it listens, type what you'd say here and press Enter.

15 seconds to start typing, 30 seconds at most (the knob allows 5 and 8 for speaking). Enter or a tap on the screen ends it.

Using it

Touch
Click, drag and flick on the screen, as with a finger
Turn
Drag the outer ring, scroll over the knob, or use the ← → keys
Haptics
Phones vibrate for each tick
Clock
Comes on after a minute untouched while nothing plays

Playback is simulated, as on the knob today. Flick speed and glide feel will be tuned on the new hardware.
